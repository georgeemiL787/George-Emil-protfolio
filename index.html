CURIOUS BY NATURE.
Engineer by training.

I am a Computer Engineering graduate from the Faculty of Engineering at Shoubra, Benha University. My strongest work sits where machine learning meets practical engineering: training and evaluating models, exposing inference through APIs, packaging services with Docker, and building interfaces that make AI systems usable.
